Deep of Night Meta Transition Guide — Post-Patch Builds

Published: July 3, 2026 · Updated for Pre-Deep of Night Patch

If you booted up Elden Ring Nightreign after the Pre-Deep of Night patch and felt like your favorite build suddenly underperforms — you're not imagining things. The June 2026 Pre-Deep of Night update rebalanced every single Nightfarer, tweaked Everdark Sovereign bosses, and overhauled several Relic interactions. This guide walks you through exactly what changed and how to adapt your build for the new meta. For complete class rankings, see our updated Nightfarer Tier List.

What the Pre-Deep of Night Patch Actually Changed

Unlike the January 1.03.2 patch that mainly buffed the bottom-tier Nightfarers (Raider, Revenant, Guardian), the June update reshuffled the entire board. According to Arcader News' coverage, the patch touched all six characters alongside several Everdark Sovereign encounters. The change log is dense, but the key takeaways for builders are:

  • Raider: Base damage multiplier increased by approximately 30%. Health scaling improved by roughly 20%. The old Berserker risk-reward now tilts heavily in your favor. Full details on IGN.
  • Guardian: Shield defense rating boosted by about 25%. Healing aura radius expanded by 40%. The support tank can now frontline more effectively.
  • Ironeye: Long-range damage reduced by 12%. Close-range shotgun spread tightened by 15%. The sniper is now encouraged to play closer.
  • Wylder: Minor adjustments only — grapple-hook cooldown reduced by 2 seconds. Still the safest all-rounder.
  • Revenant: Skill cooldown reduced by 18%. Summon duration extended by 8 seconds. The summoner class got meaningful quality-of-life improvements.
  • Executor: Execution damage threshold increased from 30% to 35% HP. Relic proc chance improved by 10% across all relics.
Key Takeaway: The gap between top and bottom Nightfarers has narrowed significantly. If you've been maining Wylder or Ironeye out of habit, now is the perfect time to experiment with Raider or Guardian.

5-Step Transition Plan for Your Current Build

Step 1: Identify Your Current Build's Role

Start by asking: is your build damage-focused, support-focused, or hybrid? The patch rewards specialization more than ever. A hybrid build that was decent at everything may now feel outclassed by pure damage dealers (Raider, Executor) or pure supports (Guardian, Revenant).

Step 2: Check Your Nightfarer's New Power Curve

Refer to our Nightfarer Tier List 2026 for the post-patch rankings. If your main dropped a tier, don't panic — the tier list shows that B-tier builds can still clear runs with the right relic setup. But if you're optimizing for speedruns or high-score runs, consider switching.

Step 3: Rebalance Your Relic Loadout

The Pre-Deep of Night patch adjusted Relic proc rates across the board. The old "stack Flat HP on Guardian" strategy is no longer optimal. Instead, test these Relic configurations:

  • Raider: 2x Crit Damage + 1x Attack Speed. The base damage buff makes crit scaling more valuable than flat attack.
  • Guardian: 1x Shield Recovery + 1x Healing Aura + 1x Max HP. The expanded aura radius makes area-of-effect healing relics much more impactful.
  • Ironeye: 1x Close Range + 1x Reload Speed + 1x Status Effect. With the range nerf, close-range damage relics compensate nicely.
  • Executor: 2x Execution Damage + 1x Relic Proc Chance. The higher execution threshold (35%) makes every proc count more.

Step 4: Adjust Your Weapon Loadout

The weapon meta hasn't shifted as dramatically, but there are notable changes. The Great Hammer family benefits from the Guardian's shield buff (more guard counters). Spears remain the top choice for Ironeye. Dual-wielding daggers on Raider is now competitive with two-handed greatswords for the first time since launch. For detailed weapon stats, check Icy Veins' Deep of Night coverage.

Step 5: Practice Against the Updated Everdark Sovereign Bosses

Several Everdark Sovereign encounters received adjustments in the June patch. The Mostema fight now has a tighter DPS window in Phase 2 — the stagger threshold was increased by 15%. The Oraczen's ranged attacks have reduced tracking, making Ironeye more viable. For detailed boss strategies, check our Boss Weakness Strategy Guide.

Meta Winners and Losers

Biggest Winners

  • Raider: Went from a high-risk meme pick to a serious contender. The 30% base damage boost makes aggressive playstyles far more rewarding.
  • Guardian: The expanded aura radius and shield buff turned this Nightfarer into the best support in the game — especially in co-op runs.
  • Revenant: The reduced skill cooldown means you can keep summon pressure up almost constantly in long boss fights.

Notable Changes

  • Ironeye: Dropped from S-tier to solid B-tier. Still viable, but requires more careful positioning and close-range play.
  • Wylder: Still the best beginner pick but has fallen behind specialized picks at high-level play.
  • Executor: More consistent than ever thanks to the execution threshold buff. A quiet winner of the patch.

Recommended Builds to Try First

Aggro Raider (Best for Solo Speedruns)

  • Weapons: Dual-wield daggers or Great Hammer
  • Relics: 2x Crit Damage, 1x Attack Speed
  • Playstyle: Relentless aggression. Stack damage as high as possible. Use grapple-hook to close gaps instantly.

Bulwark Guardian (Best for Co-op Teams)

  • Weapons: Great Hammer + Shield
  • Relics: 1x Shield Recovery, 1x Healing Aura, 1x Max HP
  • Playstyle: Keep the aura up, guard counter frequently, and let your teammates focus on DPS.

Final Thoughts

The Pre-Deep of Night patch is one of the most consequential updates Nightreign has seen. It rewards aggressive play, deepens the support role, and closes the power gap between Nightfarers. If you haven't updated your build since January's 1.03.2 patch, the transition might feel jarring at first — but once you adapt, the game feels more balanced and more fun.
