Nightfarer Tier List 2026 — Best Post-Patch Characters

Published: July 3, 2026 · Updated for Pre-Deep of Night Patch

The Pre-Deep of Night patch in June 2026 shook up Elden Ring Nightreign's meta more than any update since launch. The old S-tier picks have shifted, the bottom-tier characters have climbed, and the overall balance is tighter than ever. This tier list ranks all six Nightfarers based on their post-patch performance across solo runs, co-op play, and speedrunning. Polygon covered the 1.03.2 update's impact on Nightfarers back in January, but the June patch changes are even more consequential.

For a deeper explanation of what changed and how to adapt your existing builds, check our Post-Patch Build Transition Guide.

🟡 S-Tier — Meta Defining

Wylder — The All-Rounder (Previously S, Still S)

Patch impact: Minor. Grapple-hook cooldown reduced by 2 seconds. No other meaningful changes.

Why S-Tier: Wylder's toolkit remains the most forgiving in the game. The grapple-hook mobility, built-in AoE damage, and versatility make it the safest choice for any content. The cooldown buff is small but noticeable — it means more frequent repositioning in boss fights and faster map traversal in the day cycle.

Best for: Beginners, solo players, anyone who wants one character that can handle everything.

Executor — The Executioner (Previously A, Now S)

Patch impact: Execution damage threshold increased from 30% to 35% HP. Relic proc chance improved by 10% across all relics.

Why S-Tier: The threshold buff is deceptively powerful. In practice, a 5% HP increase on the execute range means builds can now trigger executes on enemies that previously required an extra hit cycle. Combined with the universal Relic proc buff, Executor has become the most consistent boss-killer in the game. Our Relic Build Guide has updated loadouts for the new patch.

Best for: Experienced players, boss rush, solo challenges.

🔵 A-Tier — Strong Picks

Raider — The Berserker (Previously C, Now A)

Patch impact: Base damage multiplier +30%. Health scaling +20%. Massive turnaround.

Why A-Tier: Raider was the biggest winner of the patch. The 30% base damage boost transforms it from a glass cannon that breaks itself into a genuine powerhouse. The health scaling buff (+20%) means you can afford to trade hits more aggressively. Dual-wielding daggers on Raider now competes with two-handed greatsword builds, giving it flexibility that didn't exist before.

Best for: Aggressive players, speedruns, solo play.

Guardian — The Support Tank (Previously B, Now A)

Patch impact: Shield defense +25%. Healing aura radius +40%. The support tank got real teeth.

Why A-Tier: The expanded aura radius is a game-changer for co-op. Before the patch, Guardian's healing aura barely covered your immediate teammates. Now it covers a much wider area, making it viable in chaotic multi-enemy encounters. The shield defense buff means Guardian can frontline against harder-hitting bosses without getting staggered.

Best for: Co-op teams, defensive play, support mains.

🟢 B-Tier — Viable With the Right Build

Revenant — The Summoner (Previously C, Now B)

Patch impact: Skill cooldown -18%. Summon duration +8 seconds.

Why B-Tier: Revenant is now genuinely fun to play. The reduced cooldown means you can maintain near-constant summon pressure in long boss fights, and the extended duration means fewer re-summon windows where you're vulnerable. It's still the hardest Nightfarer to master, but the skill ceiling is now worth climbing.

Best for: Tactical players, co-op support, those willing to learn a complex kit.

Ironeye — The Ranged Executioner (Previously S, Now B)

Patch impact: Long-range damage -12%. Close-range shotgun spread +15% tighter.

Why B-Tier: Ironeye took the biggest hit in the patch. The 12% long-range damage reduction is significant — key breakpoints that used to two-shot elite enemies now require three shots. The tighter shotgun spread helps in close quarters, but it doesn't fully compensate for the lost sniping power. Still viable, but requires better positioning and closer engagement than before.

Best for: Players who love the sniper fantasy and don't mind adapting to close-range play.

Patch Comparison: Before vs After

  • Wylder: S → S (stable, minor cooldown buff)
  • Executor: A → S (execution threshold + relic proc buff)
  • Raider: C → A (massive damage + health buffs)
  • Guardian: B → A (shield + aura buffs)
  • Revenant: C → B (cooldown + duration buffs)
  • Ironeye: S → B (range damage nerf)
Bottom Line: The meta is healthier than ever. The gap between the best and worst Nightfarers has narrowed dramatically. In the January 1.03.2 meta, Wylder and Ironeye dominated while Raider and Guardian struggled. Now, five of six characters are genuinely competitive, and even Ironeye can perform with the right build.

Which Nightfarer Should You Pick?

  • First time playing? Wylder is still the best starting point. Refer to our Beginner's Guide for the full tutorial.
  • Speedrunning? Raider or Executor — both benefit heavily from the patch's damage scaling changes.
  • Co-op with friends? Guardian is the most valuable team player right now. Pair with a Raider or Executor for maximum synergy.
  • Want a challenge? Ironeye B-tier runs are actually more engaging than S-tier runs — learning the new close-range playstyle is rewarding.
