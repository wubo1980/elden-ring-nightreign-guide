How to Level Up Fast: Elden Ring Nightreign Runes & Progression Guide

Progression Guide  ·  Updated May 2026

The Rune Economy in Nightreign

Runes in Nightreign function identically to the main Elden Ring game — they are the universal currency for leveling up, purchasing items, and upgrading equipment. The difference is that each run is a self-contained cycle: runes do not persist between runs. What you earn in a run stays in that run unless spent.

This creates a specific strategic pressure: you must balance spending runes immediately (investing in power to earn more runes) against saving them (stacking for a single powerful level-up session before a boss). The answer varies by phase, and this guide covers every stage of the run economy.

How Rune Costs Scale

Rune costs per level follow a curve — the higher your current level, the more runes each subsequent level costs. The curve is aggressive enough that spreading points evenly across all stats feels wasteful.

Level Range Runes per Level Notes
1–10 ~400–600 Cheap levels. Spend freely. Every point here meaningfully extends your survivability window.
11–25 ~700–1,200 Transition zone. Focus on your primary scaling stat. Diminishing returns begin here.
26–40 ~1,400–2,500 Heavy investment zone. Each level takes significant time. Prioritize weapon upgrades over raw stats above 30.
41–60 ~2,800–5,000 Late game. Marginal gains per level. Focus on boss rune drops and farm routes rather than grinding mobs.

Where to Find Runes Fast

Early Game Farm: The Sunken Cross Waypoint

The immediate area around the first Site of Grace (the Sunken Cross) contains a patrol route of low-density hollow enemies that respawn every 30 seconds. A full circuit of the area yields approximately 800–1,200 runes per minute of farming. It is not glamorous, but it is consistent and requires no risk.

Route: Cross Patrol Loop

~900–1,200 runes / circuit

Start at grace → cross the bridge (2 hollow archers) → loop left through the collapsed chapel (3 melee hollows) → drop down to the lower crypt (1 elite hollow with larger drop) → return to grace. Respawn timer: 30 seconds. Time per circuit: 45–60 seconds.

Mid Game Farm: The Bleeding Hollow Gauntlet

By the time you reach The Bleeding Hollow area, you have enough HP to survive patrol mistakes. The most efficient mid-game farm is the area's elite patrol — a four-enemy group that includes a Knight and three lesser soldiers. The Knight alone drops 600–900 runes, and the soldiers add another 400. Combined with the ambient mob drops along the route, a full clear of this area yields 3,500–4,500 runes.

Route: The Bleeding Hollow Elite Clear

~3,500–4,500 runes / clear

From the area Site of Grace, progress left through the outer courtyard (ambient mob drops ~600 runes) → enter the inner courtyard where the Knight patrols with three soldiers → clear all four → rest at the inner bonfire to reset the patrol. This is the best runes-per-minute farm in the game's first half.

Late Game Farm: Night Altar Rune Circle

The area leading up to the Night Keeper boss contains a circular room with a rune sigil on the floor. Standing in the sigil for 10 seconds activates it, releasing a burst of spectral runes worth 2,000–3,000 depending on your current level. It activates once per run and once per rest at the nearby Site of Grace. This is the single highest guaranteed rune source in a single location.

Location: Night Altar Sigil Chamber

~2,000–3,000 runes / activation

Located in the chamber before the Night Keeper fog wall. Step into the glowing rune circle on the floor. Wait 10 seconds for the full channel. Each Site of Grace rest resets it — meaning you can activate it twice per run if you rest at the altar grace before triggering the boss fight.

Boss Rune Drops

Every boss in Nightreign drops a fixed rune amount on defeat, scaled to their difficulty tier. Do not skip boss attempts to farm mobs — the rune-to-time ratio on boss kills is almost always better than any mob farm, especially for later bosses.

  • Hollow Warden: ~2,000 runes
  • Sir Gavain: ~3,500 runes
  • Night Keeper: ~5,000 runes
  • Dorthagor: ~7,000 runes
  • Nightreign Incarnade: ~12,000 runes

Level Spending Strategy

For Damage Dealers (Wraith, Duelist, Berserker)

Prioritize your primary scaling stat above all else. For Wraith: Intelligence. For Duelist: Dexterity. For Berserker: Strength. Every point in a non-scaling stat below 20 is a wasted opportunity. Get your primary stat to 35 before touching secondary stats. Your class skill damage scales with your primary stat — getting it to 35 is the first major power spike.

First priority: Primary stat to 35 > Weapon +3 upgrade > Secondary stat to 20 > HP to 40 > Weapon +5 upgrade > Everything else.

For Supports (Paladin, Cleric)

Faith is your primary scaling stat, but survivability is your job. Invest in Vigor first to a minimum of 25 — below 25 Vigor, any boss phase-two burst will one-shot you. After Vigor 25, push Faith to 30, then return to Vigor toward 35. Holy damage spells scale with Faith, but they scale with nothing if you are dead.

For Summoners (Conjurer, Grave Keeper)

Intelligence for Conjurer, Faith for Grave Keeper. Summons themselves scale with your primary casting stat. Critically: do not neglect Stamina. Summoners who run out of stamina mid-cast lose their summon window — and the summon's aggro window is the most valuable asset in the fight. Keep Stamina at 25 minimum before investing elsewhere.

Weapon Upgrade Priority vs. Raw Levels

A common beginner mistake is dumping runes entirely into character levels while ignoring weapon upgrades. Weapon upgrade scaling in Nightreign is separate from level scaling and is often more impactful per rune spent.

A rough rule of thumb: a +3 weapon at level 20 will outperform a +0 weapon at level 30 in most encounters. Prioritize at least +3 on your primary weapon before pushing levels past 25. A fully upgraded weapon at +8 or +10 in the late game is the difference between a three-minute boss kill and a six-minute boss kill.

The Rune Death Trap

Every rune you earn is on a countdown. When you die, you drop every rune you are carrying in a visible pile at the site of your death. If you die again before recovering them, they disappear. This is the single most punishing economy mechanic in Nightreign.

How to Avoid the Death Spiral

  1. Spend runes aggressively. Do not sit on a large rune pile. The moment you accumulate enough for a meaningful level or upgrade, spend it. A rune in a stat is safe. A rune in your inventory is a risk.
  2. Mark your death locations. If you die and have a large rune pile, make a mental note or set a quick in-game marker. Coming back for a 5,000-rune pile is worth the detour.
  3. Use the grace before boss attempts. If you are about to fight a boss and you are carrying more than 3,000 runes, spend them first. Dying mid-boss with a large rune pile is a catastrophic setback.

Multi-Run Progression: Building Power Across Cycles

Nightreign is a roguelike. Progression is not linear — each run resets your stats but not your knowledge. The goal across multiple runs is to reach higher levels faster each cycle by optimizing the same efficient paths you learn through repetition.

By run three, you should know the Cross Patrol Loop and the Bleeding Hollow Elite Clear by memory. By run five, the Night Altar Sigil activation should be automatic. By run seven, the Incarnade boss fight should feel navigable rather than overwhelming.

Knowledge compounds. The fastest players in Nightreign are not the ones who grind the most — they are the ones who waste the least time on inefficient routes. Cut the fat, run the routes in this guide, and invest runes immediately. Progression will take care of itself.
