How Weapon Upgrading Works

Weapon upgrading in Elden Ring Nightreign follows a similar system to the base game but with adjusted material curves and some key differences introduced in Patch 1.0.3.2 (Deep of Night). Each weapon has a maximum upgrade level determined by its weapon type: standard melee and ranged weapons can reach +9, while special weapons (boss weapons, unique drops) max out at +6.

For reference, the Bandai Namco's official Elden Ring page has a detailed breakdown of the mechanics mentioned here.

Upgrading increases base damage, stat scaling, and in some cases adds hidden effects like improved guard boost or stamina efficiency. The cost in runes and smithing stones increases with each level, making it important to plan which weapons to upgrade rather than spreading materials across too many options.

The total cost to upgrade a standard weapon from +0 to +9 is 54 smithing stones (across all four tiers) and approximately 35,000 runes. Special weapons use Somber Smithing Stones with a different cost curve: +0 to +6 requires a total of 12 Somber Stones and roughly 24,000 runes.

💡 Key insight: Smithing Stone [4] is the bottleneck in the mid-to-late game. Each upgrade from +6 requires more stones, and [4] drops are rarer than lower tiers. Prioritize one weapon to +7 before upgrading others.

Standard vs Special Weapons

Standard weapons (straight swords, greatswords, spears, bows, etc.) upgrade to +9 using regular smithing stones. They offer more flexibility because you can change ashes of war and affinities, but require more total stones to reach maximum level.

Special weapons (boss remembrance weapons, unique drops, legendary armaments) upgrade to +6 using Somber Smithing Stones. They cannot change ashes of war or affinities, but they cost fewer total stones and often have unique weapon arts that make them competitive at lower investment.

For most builds, the recommendation is to upgrade one standard weapon early (to +3), then pivot to a special weapon if you find one that matches your build. Special weapons reach meaningful power levels faster, which matters more in Nightreign's condensed progression.

Material Farming Locations

Smithing Stone [1] and [2]: Dropped by miners in the Limgrave tunnels and sold by the Roundtable Hold merchant after giving them the first miner's bell bearing. These are common enough in the early game that you should not need to farm them deliberately.

Smithing Stone [3]: Found in the Liurnia crystal tunnels, dropped by miners in Altus Plateau, and sold after finding the second miner's bell bearing in the Sealed Tunnel. These become available naturally around the mid-game transition.

Smithing Stone [4]: Limited early-game drops from the Giant-Conquering Hero's Grave and the Mountaintops miners. The merchant bell bearing for Tier 4 is found in the Dragonbarrow Cave, which is accessible mid-game but has dangerous enemies. This is the tier where dedicated farming becomes worthwhile.

Somber Smithing Stones: Found near key boss arenas and in select mines with glowing, dark ore. The Somberstone Miner's Bell Bearings are in later areas, so early special weapons should be upgraded sparingly until you unlock farming.

Optimal Upgrade Route

Early game (Limgrave / Weeping Peninsula): Prioritize one standard weapon to +3. This costs 12 smithing stones [1] and [2] — all of which can be found in the first few hours without farming. A +3 weapon is sufficient for all early game content.

Mid game (Liurnia / Caelid): Push your primary weapon to +6 (requires [3] stones). If you have found a strong special weapon, consider investing Somber Stones to bring it to +4. The mid-game is where weapon damage starts to matter more than raw stats, especially for boss encounters.

Late game (Altus / Mountaintops): Push to +9 on your primary weapon. Farm [4] stones if needed. This investment pays off immediately in the harder zones. Secondary weapons can stay at +6.

End game / New Game Plus: Max out one special weapon and one standard weapon. The special weapon handles unique situations while the standard weapon provides flexibility with ashes of war and affinities.

What Changed in Patch 1.0.3.2

The Deep of Night patch (March 5, 2026) made two significant changes to weapon upgrading. First, the cost for [4] smithing stones at higher upgrade levels was reduced. Previously, +7 to +8 required 10 stones; now it requires 8. +8 to +9 required 12; now it requires 10. This makes the endgame upgrade path noticeably cheaper.

Second, Somber Smithing Stone drop rates were increased in mid-game areas. Players can now reliably find Somber [3] and [4] in the Altus Plateau mines without needing to reach the Mountaintops. This makes special weapons more viable for mid-game builds.
